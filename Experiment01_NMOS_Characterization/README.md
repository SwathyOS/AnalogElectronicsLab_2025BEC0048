# Experiment 01 - NMOS Characterization

## Aim

This experiment focuses on the characterization of an NMOS transistor by studying its DC operating point and current-voltage behavior.

## Design Specifications

- NMOS transistor characterization
- DC operating point analysis
- ID-VDS characteristics
- Study of NMOS behavior under different bias conditions

## Circuit Description

An NMOS transistor circuit was prepared in Cadence Virtuoso for characterization. The circuit was used to observe the transistor's DC operating point and its current-voltage characteristics under different gate and drain voltage conditions.

## Simulation Procedure

1. Prepared the NMOS schematic and simulation setup.
2. Performed the DC operating point analysis to observe the transistor bias point.
3. Recorded the DC operating point values of VGS, VD, and ID.
4. Performed the IV characteristic analysis to study the drain current behavior for different gate and drain voltages.
5. Saved the relevant screenshots and plots in the corresponding folders.

## Results

The following results were obtained during the experiment:

- DC operating point values of VGS, VD, and ID.
- ID-VDS characteristic plots for the NMOS transistor.
- Screenshots of the simulation results.

The corresponding screenshots and plots are uploaded in the respective folders of this repository.

## Observations

- The DC operating point screenshot shows the bias condition of the NMOS, including the values of VGS, VD, and ID.
- The IV characteristic plots demonstrate that the drain current increases as the gate voltage is increased.
- The current response also shows the expected dependence on drain voltage, with the device showing a transition from linear to saturation-like behavior as the bias conditions change.

## Conclusion

The NMOS transistor was successfully characterized through DC operating point analysis and IV characteristic analysis. The obtained results demonstrate the dependence of drain current on the gate and drain voltages and provide an understanding of the operating behavior of the NMOS transistor.
